Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from Grafonia.

How long does delivery take?

Most standard orders are ready within 48 hours. Business cards typically take 24 hours. Large format prints may take up to 3 business days. Urgent orders can be expedited — just let us know on WhatsApp.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept PDF (preferred), AI, PSD, CDR, EPS, PNG and JPEG files. For best results, provide files at 300 DPI or higher in CMYK color mode. Don't worry — our team will guide you if needed.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer and cash on delivery (Dhaka only). 50% advance payment is required for new orders, with the balance due upon delivery.

Can you design for me if I have no file?

Absolutely! Our in-house design team will create your design from scratch. Share your brand details, preferred colors, and any references. A free initial consultation is included with every print order.

How do I ensure the print quality is correct?

We send a digital proof for your approval before printing. For color-critical orders, we can provide a physical sample print. All our machines are calibrated daily to ensure CMYK accuracy.

Do you deliver outside Dhaka?

Yes! We deliver to all 64 districts of Bangladesh via courier services (Pathao, Sundarban, SA Paribahan). Dhaka customers enjoy same-day express delivery options. Delivery charge varies by location.
